About The Rodriguez Co

A Family Builder Dedicated To Your Vision

Family-first craftsmanship for roofing, remodeling, repairs, and property improvements across Pearland, Houston, and surrounding communities.

The Rodriguez Co was forged on a simple family-first ethos: deliver premium, robust construction workmanship built correctly the first time without exception.

Operating straight out of Pearland, Texas, our mobile construction team serves homeowners and commercial properties across Houston, Friendswood, League City, and beyond. We prioritize clear communication, clean job sites, detailed project scopes, and dependable material selection.

From storm siding repairs and asphalt roof installation down to custom kitchen drywalling, tiling, plumbing swaps, painting, and flooring, our family group keeps the homeowner process unified. You map the vision; we execute the craftsmanship.
